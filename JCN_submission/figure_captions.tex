% 9
Measured parameter sensitivity of the maximum refinement iterations $K$ (reduced-order reimplementation, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct). (a) Task Accuracy does \emph{not} plateau at $K=3$: Level 2 (a single deterministic run, confirmed identical on repeat) keeps improving through $K=5$; Level 3 (averaged over 3 masking seeds) plateaus in only 1 of 3 seeds individually. (b) Token overhead per consolidated episode is concave, not linear: most of the cost increase happens between $K{=}1$ and $K{=}2$, with sharply diminishing marginal cost for extending the budget further, since only a minority of episodes ever use the additional iterations.
